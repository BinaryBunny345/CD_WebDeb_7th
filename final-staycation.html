-Alpine Loop-

One of the most famous things to do in American Fork Canyon is to drive the Alpine Loop. A 20-mile scenic road connecting American Fork Canyon (SR-92) to Provo Canyon (US-189) over an 8,000-foot summit. It is open from late May through late October and is closed to the public in the winter due to snow. One reason it is the most visited drive in Utah is because it has vibrant fall leaves and amazing views of Mount Timpanogos, as well as passing by Sundance Resort and Cascade Springs.

There are places that you can go in American Fork Canyon to go camping to make your trip a little longer! These campsites have firepits, grills, restrooms, tables, and parking spots for your cars. There is a nightly fee for developed campgrounds, the prices often start around $27 to $31+ per night. Camping in a dispersed campsite is free, but there will not be any facilities or services provided.
