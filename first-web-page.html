History, Pros, and Cons of Baking

Baking is a great way to releive stress, get something yummy out the process, and connect with others.
It is said that baking had been around almost for 14,400 years! Baking is best known for creating breads, cakes, cookies, pastries, and pies using dry heat.
A study published by Journal of Positive Psychology states that people who bake often feel more relaxed and happier in general because of
the small creative projects they are doing while baking.

A fun fact about baking, is that chocolate chip cookies were made by accident by a man named Ruth Graves who was just trying to melt a chocolate bar!
While baking, there are many different aspects to the process. It is improtant to get the flour and sugar ratios right, it's improtant to bake the treat for the correct amount of time and in the right temperature. I'm not going to lie to you, there are some cons about baking as well, such as low margin for errors, or how time-consuming it can be, but over time it becomes the highlight of my day.

Baking Ideas

There are many different types of sweet treats to bake! For instance you could bake a moist chocolate cake, soft and chewy snickerdoodles, or even a hot batch of brownies! Baking allows your creativity to run wild by chosing a theme to theme your desserts on, bringing comfort and nostalgia to your kitchen, and even impressing all of your friends. Creating lemon bars or fruit bars brings more of a refreshing vibe, but making fudgy browneis with dark chocolate chips stuffed inside is more of a comfort treat. Allowing yourself to get in the kitchen and make a little mess is the best way to fix your mood and share your talent with others!
